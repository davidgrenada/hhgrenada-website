The Culinary Experience

A 3.5-hour journey into the heart of Grenadian cuisine

Hosted at our family home in Mount Parnassus, you'll walk our 1.25-acre farm, harvest ingredients straight from the soil, learn to cook Grenada's most beloved dishes, and sit down to a three-course meal on a terrace that overlooks the hills.

Led by Chef Kennedy — whose passion for Grenadian culture, history and seasonal cooking makes every conversation as nourishing as the food itself — this is an experience that feels less like a class and more like being welcomed into the family.
